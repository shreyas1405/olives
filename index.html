La Nostra Storia • Our Story

Where Italy Meets the Garden City

Herbs n Olives is a reading café for Italian and Mexican comfort food in Ideal Homes Township, Rajarajeshwari Nagar. Our pizzas bake in a wood-fired oven, our pastas are built around fragrant basil, oregano and good olive oil, and every table is meant to be lingered at.

Step inside and you will find painted walls, weathered blue shutters and flowering bougainvillea: a small corner of the Mediterranean in the middle of the city.

Bring a book, open a laptop or simply linger over coffee and pasta. This is a café that is happy to let you stay.
