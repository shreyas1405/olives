La Nostra Storia · Our Story

A little courtyard of the Mediterranean, tucked away in RR Nagar

❧

Some places are made for hurrying. Herbs n Olives was made for the opposite.

Turn into the quiet 12th Cross of Ideal Homes Township and you'll find a café that feels a long way from the city around it. Painted walls glow in warm afternoon light. Weathered blue shutters and a carved old door look out over climbing bougainvillea. Somewhere behind it all, a wood-fired oven crackles. We wanted Herbs n Olives to feel like a courtyard in a small Italian town: unhurried, generous and a little romantic.

Our kitchen is simple, and it is honest. Every pizza is stretched by hand and baked in the live fire of our wood-fired oven until the crust is blistered, smoky and tender. Our pastas are tossed to order in sauces built from ripe tomatoes, basil, garlic and good olive oil. Alongside our Italian favourites sit the bright, generous flavours of Mexico: nachos, sizzlers and plenty of lime. Every dish we serve is vegetarian, and every one is made to be shared.

We are also, quietly, a reading café. You'll find books on the shelves and corners made for lingering, so bring a novel, a friend or a laptop. Order a cappuccino, then another, and let the afternoon go slowly. Families come for long Sunday lunches, and friends come for unhurried dinners. Plenty of guests come alone, with nothing but a book and an appetite.

We think a meal should be remembered for the people you shared it with, and for the plate itself. We look forward to welcoming you to our table.
